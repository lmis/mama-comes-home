# Fox Family — prototype asset bundle

These are PNG reference sheets and animation frame sheets, **not production-ready GIFs or individual sprites**.

## Contents
- reference/character_reference.png: design, expressions, props, pose overview (paper background; visual reference only).
- reference/locked_family_design.png: family design with Papa's ponytail and square blue glasses and Mama's circular red glasses (paper background).
- animation_sheets/papa_reaction.png: 3x3 reaction sequence on magenta.
- animation_sheets/family_reaction.png: 3x3 family animation on magenta.
- animation_sheets/twins_celebration.png: 3x3 family/twins animation on magenta.
- animation_sheets/table_scene_composite.png: 3x3 composite dining scene on magenta, NOT separated layers.
- props/home_furniture_sheet.png: multiple home objects on magenta, NOT individual extracted files.

## Caveats
- The 8 complete backgrounds and independent idle loops have NOT been produced.
- No separate depth/occlusion layers are included.
- Backgrounds may contain nonuniform magenta and textured/antialiased edges. Check and clean before chroma-keying.
- Verify all cell dimensions, consistent silhouettes, positioning, and art continuity before integration.
- Avoid using composites as separate character sprites.
- The gift contents must never be shown.
